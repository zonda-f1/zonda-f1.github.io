About Us

Founded in 2000, we began as a small mold workshop serving nearby hardware and plastic factories, mainly undertaking partial mold development projects. Through years of continuous technical advancement, we have grown into a full-process mold solution provider with comprehensive capabilities — from customer requirement analysis, mold design, mold processing, assembly, and testing, to providing complete after-sales and maintenance services. In particular, we have over 20 years of specialized R&D experience in the field of plastic water guns, with extensive expertise in fluid control and ergonomic design.

Since 2021, we have expanded into the manufacturing of finished plastic water guns. After four years of development, our factory has established mature capabilities in injection molding, assembly, and product testing, enabling full-process quality control and traceability — from steel plates to molds, and from raw plastic materials to finished products.

We offer our clients a wide range of ODM/OEM options: customization of existing products (e.g., custom colors, engraving, and patterns) as well as full-solution development — from design to mass production, including both mold development and product manufacturing.
